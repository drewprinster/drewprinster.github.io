{%- comment -%} Renders every publication, grouped by topic per _data/topics.yml. Group order comes from that file; order within a group comes from each publication's `topic_order` (papers without one fall to the end of their group). Publications whose `topic` matches no group -- or which have none at all -- are collected under "Other", so a typo cannot silently drop a paper from the page. {%- endcomment -%} {%- comment -%} Slugs as an array: `contains` then matches whole elements, not substrings. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign slug_string = "" -%} {%- for group in site.data.topics.groups -%} {%- assign slug_string = slug_string | append: group.slug | append: "|" -%} {%- endfor -%} {%- assign slug_list = slug_string | split: "|" -%} {%- for group in site.data.topics.groups -%} {%- assign docs = site.publications | where: "topic", group.slug | sort: "topic_order", "last" -%} {%- if docs.size > 0 %}
